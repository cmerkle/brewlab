# Changelog

## 0.7.1

- **The Home Screen app no longer falls back to an old version.** The service worker now finishes
  saving each fresh page before iOS stops it, so the copy it opens when the server can't be reached
  is the version you last used, not one from around when you installed it (the old copy is cleared
  once). And when the app comes back to the front or back online and the server has a different
  version, a bar says "Brewlab X.Y.Z is available" with a Reload button.

## 0.7.0

- **Grind for the roast.** Brew setup now moves the grind for the bag's roast level: finer for lighter
  roasts, coarser for darker ones, relative to the roast the recipe was written for. Recipes have a new
  optional "Written for a roast" field; recipes without one count as medium-light. Picking a Stash bag
  in the recipe editor fills an empty roast from the bag, and "Set to <bag>'s roast" replaces one
  you chose. More → Settings →
  "Grind for the roast" switches it on or off and sets the shift for each roast level (as a % of the
  grinder's range, with a worked example on your own grinder), the baseline roast and which methods it
  applies to. It's off by default; the starting values (light −5%, dark +15% of the range vs
  medium-light, and so on) are a rule of thumb, not published numbers. Brew setup's note says why the grind moved, with a one-tap
  "Use <recipe grind>". Roast and dose adjustments add up and are rounded once.

## 0.6.1

- **Tag fields** (Discover → Your taste, a bag's tasting notes): suggestions now appear as you type, matching the start of a word, right under the box — tap one, or ↓/↑ and Enter. On iPhone the full list used to pop up after pressing Enter, when the box was empty.
- **README screenshots:** Insights on a phone, Insights and the filtered Brew Log on a computer, and the Grafana dashboard (in the Prometheus section). They use the demo data plus five months of made-up brewing (`tests/e2e/screenshot-data.mjs`).
- Tests: the onboarding check for the When you brew heatmap counts the hourly grid (168 cells).

## 0.6.0

- **Brew timer: Next step now and Wait.** *Next step now* starts the next step at once (the bed drained early); *Wait* holds the next step past its time until you tap *Next step now* (the bed is still wet), showing how long you've waited. Either way the steps after it keep their durations and move with it, the step list shows the new times, and the clock keeps real time, so the logged total is what the brew took. Survives the app being closed mid-brew.
- **Insights → When you brew** is hourly: 24 one-hour columns per weekday (was 3-hour blocks), labelled every three hours.
- **Grafana dashboard:** the Brewlab wordmark across the top, in Grafana 11 and 12. It follows Grafana's theme (light text on the dark theme, dark text on the light one). Your browser loads it from the repo via jsDelivr; without internet the row stays empty. The *At a glance* tiles are wider, six to a row.

## 0.5.0

- **Brew Log filters:** a filter button inside the search box opens filters for coffee, roaster, origin, roast level, process, recipe, brew method, brewer, grinder, water, rating and dates. Pick any number of values (any of them within a group; groups combine), each showing how many brews it leaves. Active filters show as chips with ×, and the button shows how many are on. They replace the coffee / recipe / water dropdowns. The search now matches every word across the brew and its Stash bag (roaster, origin, process, tasting notes), the grind setting, dose and date.
- **Stash and Archive filters:** the same filter button in the search box, with status, rest window (resting / peak / past, from your rest settings), roaster, origin, roast level, process, tasting-note tags, rating, and date ranges for roasted, bought, opened and finished. Each tab keeps its own filters; the list says how many of the bags it's showing. Groups with a single value are left out.
- **Half-star ratings** for brews and bags (½ to 5): tap the left half of a star for x.5. Insights adds half-star columns once you use them; filters, search and metrics understand them.
- **Grafana dashboard** (`grafana/brewlab-dashboard.json`, generated by `scripts/grafana-dashboard.mjs`): 113 panels on brewing over time, when you brew, ratings and ratios, gear, recipes and water, your coffee and how you rate it, the stash bag by bag, buying coffee and costs, Discover and backups. New history metrics (by month, day, weekday × hour; purchases and costs per month; every bag by roaster / origin / roast level / process; tasting notes; method averages; how long bags last) fill it from the first scrape.
- **Prometheus metrics** for Grafana (More → Settings → Prometheus metrics, off by default): `GET /metrics` with brews and coffee used in total, by recent days and by every brew dimension; ratings, ratio and streak; the latest brew; each open or unopened bag (coffee left, days off roast, rest window state); spending; gear, recipes, waters, Discover and backups. See the README for the list and a scrape config.

## 0.4.0

Headline numbers (brews against the previous period, coffee used, average rating and ratio, coffees, bags finished, spending, brewing streak), brews per day / week / month, running totals of coffee used and money spent on beans, a weekday × hour heatmap, rating and ratio distributions, brewers / grinders / recipes / methods / waters by brews or by grams of coffee used, your coffee by roaster / origin / roast level / process (by brews in the period, bags in the Stash now, or every bag), and what you rate highest. Every chart has a table view and keyboard-reachable tooltips. On wider screens charts sit two to a row with matching heights.

## 0.3.0

- **Stash archive:** a finished bag leaves the Stash and moves to its Archive (newest first), with its brews and tasting notes kept. *Back to stash* undoes a finish.
- **Sort and search the Stash and its Archive:** sort by name, roaster, origin, process, roast / purchase / opened / finished date, rating, price or coffee left, and flip the direction with one tap. Bags missing that value go last either way. Each tab remembers its sort on the device. Search matches every word against the name, roaster, origin, process, roast level, notes, dates ("2026-10", "october", "Nov 4"), rating ("5★") and price ("$18").
- **Bag details from lists:** roast level is a list (ultra light, light, medium-light, medium, medium-dark, dark). Process and origin are lists with *Custom…* for your own words. The lists live in `catalog/coffee-vocab.json`. Roast levels written another way ("light-medium") are matched to the list.
- **Tasting-note tags** on Stash bags (strawberry, citrus …), searchable and shown on the card. Tasting notes from a bag photo, a roaster link or Discover arrive as tags. Older bags with a "Tasting notes: …" line in their notes have it moved into tags once. Tags on rated bags feed Discover's taste summary.
- **Rest and peak window per roast level** (More): days to rest and days at its best, starting from rules of thumb (ultra light 14/60 days down to dark 3/21). The Stash's freshness notes use them.
- **Discover → Your taste is all tags now:** five sections (roasters, processes, roast level, origins, tasting notes), each with *Preferred*, *Rather avoid* and *Do not include*. Tap a tag to switch it on or off, × removes it, and you can type your own. The quiz's flavours, processes and roasts (now with ultra light) and the old "always leave out" rules are built-in tags of one or two words each, all off to start (`catalog/taste-defaults.json`). Long ones are split, so the old funk rule is now Funky, Boozy, Winey, Barrel-aged and Solvent. Preferred and Rather avoid steer the research and the ranking; Do not include leaves a coffee out. Your existing rules, quiz answers and likes move over once, and what was on stays on. On wide screens the three lists sit side by side with their inputs lined up.
- **Stash sort:** "Open first" is now "Default (open, then unopened)".
- **First-run setup:** *Select all* and *Deselect all* for starter recipes (recipes you already have are never added twice). The search boxes no longer leave a big gap below them, and neither does the gear library's.
- **Reset preferences** (Discover → Your taste, and setup's Discover step): after asking, every section goes back to the built-in tags, all off. Running setup again leaves your tags alone.
- **Edit what's left in a bag** (the bag editor's *Coffee left*): for coffee used without logging a brew. Logged brews keep counting down from the number you set, and the card says how much was used without a brew.

## 0.2.0

- **Brew setup holds the ratio:** change the coffee and the water follows, change the water and the coffee follows. Only the ratio field and the ratio buttons change the ratio.
- **Water recipes** (More → Water): a base water, a volume and additives (minerals, drops, packets), with hardness and alkalinity worked out as you type, in ppm as CaCO₃. Salts are calculated from molar mass. Lotus drops and Third Wave Water use published figures, and anything unpublished shows as "—". Mark a water as a concentrate (a stock solution) and other waters add it by the gram. A cited library: the Third Wave Water profiles, Lotus Light & Bright and Rao/Perger, and Barista Hustle's concentrate recipes (the eight from its redux post, the original filter recipe and an espresso one) with their concentrates. Recipes can have a default water, Brew setup lets you pick one, brews record it, and the Brew Log filters by water and compares how each rated.
- **Brew timer:** the clock, the current step and the countdown to the next one stay at the top; the list of steps scrolls on its own and follows the brew, so the timer is never pushed off screen.

## 0.1.2

- **Backups:** delete a backup from More → Backups (it asks first), and choose how many to keep there. Lowering the number deletes the oldest ones right away, after asking. *Use the default* goes back to `BACKUP_KEEP` (14 unless you set it).
- **OXO Rapid Brewer Default** recipe: 20 g to 160 g of water (OXO's own hot recipe, about 2 minutes), not 300 g, which the brewer can't hold. Recipes you already added keep their values; edit the water there.
- **OXO Rapid Brewer Cold** recipe (new): OXO's cold recipe, 40 g to 160 g of cold water, about 5 minutes.
- **Layout:** the arrow on the rounded filter dropdowns (Recipes, Brew Log) was cut off on Windows; *Ready to brew* and *Add custom gear* are now centred under the form on wide screens instead of sitting at the left.

## 0.1.0 — first public release

**Brew**
- Recipes with a guided brew timer: *Brew setup* (dose, water, ratio, temperature, bag, grinder) → *Ready to brew* → *Start timer* when you start pouring → log the brew, with temperature and grind still editable.
- Ratio calculator with 1:13–1:17 and 1:2 quick picks; pour steps scale off the water. Grams or ounces, °C or °F.
- Grind guide and **grind converter**: a setting keeps its position inside one grinder's range for a brew method and lands at the same position on your other grinders. Brew methods run finest → coarsest: Turkish, espresso, moka pot, AeroPress, Rapid Brewer, pour-over, drip coffee maker, French press, cold brew.
- **Grind for a different dose** (More): brewing more or less than the recipe moves the grind by a set share of the range per doubling of the dose. You choose how much and for which methods, or turn it off.
- Stash of bags (derived status: unopened → open → finished, days off roast), duplicate a bag, brew log, tasting notes.
- **Bags from a photo:** take, choose, paste or drop a photo; the label is read by your AI provider or by local OCR, and with a web-search provider the coffee is looked up on the roaster's own site (fills empty fields only and never guesses). Paste a roaster link to import a bag.

**Gear, as data**
- A library of 47 grinders and brewers in `catalog/gear/*.json` and 20 recipe templates in `catalog/recipes/*.json`. Adding one gives you your own editable copy, and library gear and custom gear have exactly the same fields.
- Grinder ranges come from a **cited source** (the maker's chart or a published community chart). Gaps are estimated from the grinder's own ranges by rules that are data (`catalog/estimate-rules.json`) and marked "est.". Each method has a "can do" box, and methods a grinder can't do show "—".
- Gear editor: per-method ranges and notes, dial notation, step and divisor, where the ranges come from, estimated rows, and **Estimate the empty rows**.
- **Suggest to the library:** builds a checked library entry from your gear and opens GitHub's new-file page. Nothing is sent from your Brewlab.

**Discover**
- A weekly board that starts with popular picks and learns from your Stash ratings, brew ratings and tastings.
- Optional AI provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible server), set in the app with a write-only key. Without one, you add candidates and Brewlab filters and ranks them.
- **Always leave out:** your own rules (a name plus words to look for), starting from suggestions that are all off. An optional taste quiz, and *Your taste* shows exactly what the research is told.

**Everything else**
- First-run setup: your brewers and grinders (with a calibration helper for grinders not in the library), units, starter recipes that fit your gear, demo data (fictional), or restore an export.
- Installable app (PWA) for phones, foldables and desktops; the layout is tested on 15 viewports.
- Nightly database snapshots (newest 14 kept), back up now, and JSON export and restore.
- **More → About:** version, what's new, report a bug (version and device filled in), ask for gear, and an optional daily update check (off by default).
- One Docker container (Node 24, SQLite) for amd64 and arm64: `ghcr.io/cmerkle/brewlab`.
